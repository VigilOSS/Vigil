# Phase 5 — Verify Findings

**Read-on-phase-entry.** Read this doc when you enter Phase 5 and again on a method re-anchor. It carries the adversarial review dispatch, the triage scope, the disposition set (confirmed / upgraded / downgraded / killed / runtime-proof-owed), the root-cause grouping, dedup and chain-primitive passes, the exit gate, and the `transition-phase exploit` checkpoint. The variant/sibling pass and the Phase-Exit Review Dispatches table stay in `AGENTS.md` ("Mandatory Phase-Exit Passes").

5. **VERIFY FINDINGS** → adversarial review of Phase 4 findings before investing in exploitation. This catches false positives, severity inflation, and weak evidence early — fixing a finding after exploitation wastes far more tokens than catching it here.
   a. Fetch all active findings: `vigil assess fetch findings`
   b. Triage the review scope:
      - **Must review**: all HIGH and CRITICAL findings — these drive exploitation and dominate the report
      - **Review by escalation potential, not a fixed quota**: review the MEDIUM findings that have *escalation potential* — chain potential (pairs with another finding into a high: info-disclosure + open-redirect, SSRF + token leak, IDOR + impersonation), auth / privilege-escalation relevance, or that sit on a high-value app unit (auth boundary, object-reference-heavy API, privileged-write surface). A medium on a throwaway static surface with no chain or auth angle can wait; a medium that could become the pivot of a high cannot. Cover the distinct vuln classes present rather than clustering the review on one.
      - **Skip**: LOW and INFO findings — review cost exceeds downstream impact
   c. Dispatch `vulnerability-analyst` in review mode with `finding-verification` plus the matching vuln-class skill (`vigil assess list-skills`). The skill carries the adversarial procedure, the FP & severity-downgrade gate (`agent/references/finding-fp-and-severity-gate.md`, C1–C7) and the report-ready story gate, so the dispatch context adds only what you hold:
      - The finding slugs, titles, and full payloads being reviewed
      - Target access (the reviewer re-tests via `vigil assess kali ...`, not just reads the evidence text)
      - **Trust-boundary / threat-model summary**: which entry points are unauthenticated vs privileged, which inputs are operator-trusted (config, internal callers), and the blast-radius tiers. Build it from the operator's engagement context, the surface's `auth_context`, and recon/fingerprint intel about internal or trusted callers — sliced to the finding's host/module rather than dumped whole on large scope. The triager is overconfident without this grounding — reachability and auth-path severity calls depend on it.
   d. For each reviewed finding, the reviewer decides a disposition per its own Decision Framework
      (`agent/agents/vulnerability-analyst.md` — `PASS`/`UPGRADE`/`DOWNGRADE`/`KILL`), then the
      operator maps it to the backend disposition set and runs the matching command:
      - **Confirmed** (`PASS`) → `vigil assess finding verify <slug> --status confirmed --reason "..." --proof-receipt-id <id> --confirm-check-file <confirm-check.json>`
      - **Upgraded** (`UPGRADE`) → `vigil assess finding verify <slug> --status confirmed --new-severity <higher> --reason "..." --proof-receipt-id <id> --confirm-check-file <confirm-check.json>`. `--new-severity` rides on any terminal status, so one write re-bands and re-attests; patching severity first invalidates the verdict you are about to record.
      - **Downgraded** (`DOWNGRADE`) → `vigil assess finding verify <slug> --status downgraded --reason "..." --new-severity <lower> --proof-receipt-id <id> --confirm-check-file <confirm-check.json>`
      - **Killed** (`KILL`) → `vigil assess finding verify <slug> --status false_positive --reason "..." --counter-check-file <counter-check.json> --details-inline '{"kill_reason": "...", "kill_check": <1-5>}'`. `kill_check` is the `finding-verification` check behind the kill; that skill's "Killed" disposition maps the reviewer's `KILL Q#` onto it. Without a kill_reason the backend stores your `--reason`. A kill whose experiment could not run is `blocked`, not `false_positive`. A reviewer who cannot meet that bar withdraws the claim with `--status disputed --reason "..."` instead, which needs nothing else and still survives a later sweep.
      - **Runtime proof owed** (source/static evidence holds, the live PoC has not been run — typically a code-track finding whose `canonical_location` is `file:`) → `vigil assess finding verify <slug> --status pending_runtime_proof --reason "..." --next-test-file <next-test.json>`, built from the reviewer's decisive test: hypothesis, confirming and falsifying observations, and route hint (`vigil assess contract finding verify`). This is the handoff into Phase 6, not a park-and-forget: the finding keeps its source location, stays out of exploit-candidates, and blocks report-readiness until a runtime pass re-verifies it `confirmed`/`downgraded`. Phase 6 prepares it from `vigil assess proof-work list`.
   e. **Root-cause grouping pass.** Once verification has produced stable findings, read every finding with `vigil assess fetch findings --paginate` and group intentional root/sibling relationships with `vigil assess finding link`. The root is the finding describing the broadest attacker-controllable cause or missing control; link downstream outcomes as siblings so developers and executives see blast radius without duplicate tickets. `--effect` defaults to `eliminates_attack_path` (the sibling stays its own remediation item after the root fix), so a pure grouping pass never disturbs a verdict. Pass `--effect eliminates_finding` only when fixing the root retires the sibling entirely: that raises a claim review on a confirmed or downgraded sibling plus a `finding_claim_review_pending` report blocker, settled by an independent `claim_equivalence` decision before the report. Grouping is audited on each sibling. Confirm with `vigil assess finding list --root-cause <root-slug>` and `vigil assess check-phase-readiness --phase report`.
   f. **Dedup pass**: check for findings with overlapping `canonical_location` and similar `defect_family` across different agent prefixes. Merge the weaker into the stronger:
      `vigil assess finding merge <source-slug> <target-slug> "<reason>"`
   g. **Chain-only primitives stay in the normal finding table**: when a finding is real only as part of a proved or expected escalation path, update it with `vigil assess finding update <slug> ...` so `details.chain_required`, `details.chain_rationale`, and `details.required_followup` are populated. Do not invent a chain-specific workflow status.
   h. **Exit gate**: all HIGH/CRITICAL findings have a verification disposition. When kills substantially outnumber confirms, ask why before moving on — are Phase-4 dispatches producing findings from theory rather than evidence (`agent/rules/hunting.md` "Prove the attacker outcome"), or did the dispatch context under-specify scope? Tighten the dispatch context for future engagements rather than treating the kill count itself as a verdict.
   Transition phase: `vigil assess transition-phase exploit` (checks unverified high/critical findings).
